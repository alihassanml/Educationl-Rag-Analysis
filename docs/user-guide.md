# User guide

## Students
1. **Register** and choose *Student*. Pick a display name (the only thing other students ever see).
2. **Add material** in *Library*: upload a PDF, paste text, add a web link (page or online PDF) or a YouTube lecture. Label it (notes, textbook, past paper, teacher material, lecture). Wait until it shows *ready*.
3. **Take the diagnostic** (dashboard banner): one or two questions per topic set your starting level.
4. **Course Map:** your topics in study order. It re-routes when you do well or struggle.
5. **Tutor chat:** ask questions. Answers cite the chapter and page (or the video time) and open the source. If your material does not cover it, you get "Not found" instead of a guess. Use *Quiz me* inside the chat for quizzes.
6. **Quizzes** get harder after 80%+ and easier below 50%. After repeated struggle you get lighter steps.
7. **Exam Predictor:** upload at least two past papers (kind *past paper*, with year) to see which topics they ask about most, with the questions as evidence.
8. **Teacher style:** with teacher material uploaded, ask the tutor to answer in your teacher's style.
9. **Peer Match:** turn it on in *Settings*. You can challenge another opted-in student with the same weak topic; they see only your display name.
10. **Viva Bot:** pick a topic and press *Start exam* once. The bot asks each question out loud, listens as soon as it finishes, sends your answer when you stop talking, then asks the follow-up or the next question by itself. *Pause voice*, *Repeat question* and *Type instead* are there if you need them. Each answer has a time limit. You get a report with a rubric and citations.
11. **Reminders and slip tests:** topics due for review create at most one notification a day. A slip test is a short quiz that updates your progress.
12. **Classes:** join a teacher's class with the 8-character code to search the material they share.

## Teachers
1. Register and choose *Teacher*.
2. **Classes:** create a class, copy the join code, share any of your documents.
3. **Teacher area:** class weak topics (shown once at least 3 students have practised a topic) and each member's progress.

## Admins
Create the first admin with `python -m app.scripts.make_admin you@example.com`. Admins can open the **Evaluation Lab** to measure retrieval quality.
